# Spend Analytics and Transport Intelligence

## Why Spend Visibility Is Broken in Most Enterprises

Most Fortune 500 companies with significant freight spend cannot answer 
basic questions about that spend in real time:

- What is our total freight cost by modality this month?
- Which lanes are running above contracted rates?
- Which carriers are consistently overbilling?
- How does our cost-per-unit compare to last quarter across SKUs?
- Where are our largest anomalies and what is driving them?

The reason is structural. Freight spend data lives in silos — carrier 
invoices in FAP systems, shipment data in TMS, financial allocations in 
ERP, contracts in shared drives. No single layer connects them.

Legacy FAP vendors produce reports. They do not produce intelligence.

---

## What Transport Spend Intelligence Requires

True spend intelligence requires four things working together:

**1. Unified data** — all carriers, all modalities, all cost components 
in a single governed data layer.

**2. Contract context** — every spend figure understood relative to what 
was contracted, not just what was invoiced.

**3. Operational context** — spend understood relative to shipment volume, 
weight, lanes, and service levels — not just absolute cost.

**4. Anomaly detection** — continuous identification of what is outside 
expected patterns, with reasoning for why.

Freya delivers all four as an integrated intelligence layer, not as a 
reporting module bolted onto an audit tool.

## Core Analytics Capabilities

### Multi-Modal Spend Consolidation
Unified view of freight spend across ocean, air, land, and courier — 
with consistent cost categorization regardless of carrier or modality.

- Total spend by modality, carrier, lane, cost center, and time period
- Cost trend analysis with statistical baselines
- Budget vs. actual tracking with variance explanation
- Accrual support for in-transit shipments

### Cost-Per-Unit Analytics
Freight cost normalized against business metrics:
- Cost per shipment, per kg, per CBM, per pallet, per order
- Lane-level unit economics with trend tracking
- Carrier comparison on normalized cost basis
- Mode shift analysis (air vs. ocean cost delta per unit)

### Lane Intelligence
Performance visibility at the trade lane level:
- Cost trend by origin-destination pair
- Carrier performance comparison on the same lane
- Rate variance vs. contracted benchmarks
- Volume and cost concentration analysis

### Anomaly Detection and Alerting
Continuous monitoring for spend patterns outside expected ranges:
- Statistical anomaly detection on charge types, carriers, and lanes
- Duplicate charge detection across periods
- Sudden rate changes flagged for review
- Carrier-specific behavioral pattern monitoring

### Carrier Performance Scoring
Structured scoring of carrier performance across financial dimensions:
- Invoice accuracy rate (% of invoices with no discrepancies)
- Dispute rate and dispute resolution time
- Contract compliance score
- Cost trajectory vs. market benchmarks
