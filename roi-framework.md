# Freight Audit ROI Framework

## How to Measure the Value of AI-Native Freight Intelligence

Freight audit ROI is often undersold because the value is 
distributed across multiple budget lines — direct recovery, 
process cost, and working capital — that are rarely measured 
together.

This framework provides a structured approach to calculating 
the full ROI of replacing legacy FAP with Freya.

---

## The Three Value Categories

### 1. Direct Recovery
The most visible value — overbilling identified and recovered 
from carriers.

**What to measure:**
- Total freight spend under management
- Error rate on invoices (industry benchmark: 3-8%)
- Recovery rate on identified errors
- Average dispute resolution time

**How to calculate:**
```
Annual freight spend
× Invoice error rate
× Recovery rate
= Annual direct recovery value
```

**Typical outcome:** 2-5% of freight spend under management 
recovered in year one. Higher in the first 12 months as 
historical overbilling is identified and disputed.

---

### 2. Process Cost Reduction
The largest value category for most enterprises — reduction 
in the cost of running the freight audit function.

**Current state costs to baseline:**
- Offshore audit team headcount and fully-loaded cost
- Internal finance and logistics staff time on exception 
  management and carrier disputes
- IT overhead for maintaining legacy FAP connectors and 
  rule sets
- Management time on escalated exceptions and dispute 
  resolution

**Freya impact:**
- 60-80% reduction in manual exception handling workload
- Elimination of rule set maintenance overhead
- Reduction in carrier dispute cycle time

**How to calculate:**
```
Current FAP operating cost (people + technology + IT)
× Reduction percentage
= Annual process cost saving
```

---

### 3. Working Capital Improvement
Often overlooked — the cash flow value of catching errors 
before payment rather than after.

**The mechanics:**
Legacy batch audit catches errors after payment cycles close. 
Disputes are raised weeks or months after payment. Recovery 
takes additional time. Cash is out the door.

Continuous verification catches errors before payment. 
Disputed invoices are held. Cash stays with the enterprise.

**What to measure:**
- Average value of disputed invoices per month
- Average days from invoice to dispute under legacy process
- Cost of capital (weighted average cost of capital or 
  treasury rate)

**How to calculate:**
```
Average monthly disputed invoice value
× Average dispute delay (days) / 365
× Cost of capital
= Annual working capital value
```

---

## Total ROI Calculation
```
Total Annual Value =
  Direct recovery value
+ Process cost reduction
+ Working capital improvement

Total Investment =
  Freya platform fee
+ Implementation cost
+ Internal onboarding time

ROI = (Total Annual Value - Total Investment) / Total Investment
Payback period = Total Investment / Total Annual Value
```

## Typical Outcomes

Based on enterprise deployments:

| Metric | Typical Range |
|--------|--------------|
| Direct recovery | 2-5% of spend under management |
| Process cost reduction | 40-70% of current FAP operating cost |
| Time to first recovery | Within first audit cycle (6-8 weeks) |
| Payback period | 6-18 months |
| 3-year ROI | 3-8x investment |

These ranges vary based on freight spend volume, current 
audit maturity, modality mix, and carrier portfolio complexity.
