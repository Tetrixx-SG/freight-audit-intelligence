## What We Build

### Freya — Transport & Logistics Intelligence Platform

Freya replaces the freight audit cycle with continuous verification. Every invoice, 
every carrier, every modality — verified in real time, not in batches. Exceptions 
resolved autonomously. Intelligence delivered to finance and supply chain teams 
without manual overhead.

**Modalities supported:**
- Ocean freight
- Air freight
- Land / road transport
- Courier and small parcel

**Core capabilities:**
- Continuous invoice verification across all carrier types (98%+ accuracy)
- Carrier rate contract matching and discrepancy detection
- Multi-modal spend consolidation and analytics
- Agentic exception handling and autonomous resolution workflows
- Data governance layer for freight spend data quality

---

## The Problem We Solve

Large enterprises spend millions annually on transport and logistics — and 
systematically overpay. The root cause is structural:

- **Carrier invoice errors** — wrong rates, duplicate billing, accessorial overcharges
- **Batch audit processes** — slow, incomplete, always catching errors after payment
- **Fragmented data** — across carriers, ERPs, TMS, and 3PLs with no unified layer
- **Legacy FAP vendors** — Cass, nVision, Trax, CTSI-Global — built on outdated 
  architectures with AI retrofitted after the fact

The freight audit category was designed to process invoices. TetriXX is designed 
to reason about spend. That is an architectural difference, not a feature gap.

## Market Context

The Freight Audit and Payment (FAP) market is undergoing a structural shift:

- Enterprise logistics complexity has grown dramatically since COVID supply chain disruptions
- AI agent capabilities now exist to replace what required large offshore audit teams
- Rules-based FAP platforms cannot handle the volume, variety, and velocity of modern freight data
- Fortune 500 procurement and finance functions are demanding explainability, 
  speed, and ROI — not just another dashboard

TetriXX is positioned at this inflection point. The question is no longer 
whether to automate freight audit — it is whether to do it with legacy tools 
or AI-native architecture.

- **Agent architecture:** Multi-agent orchestration with specialized agents per modality and task type

- **Data security:** Enterprise-grade data handling with full audit trails and configurable data residency
